# 502 people died on Indian roads today.

Yesterday too. Tomorrow too — unless something changes.

Something is changing.

**From October 1, 2028, every new vehicle sold in India must be able to talk to every other vehicle on the road.** Cars. Buses. Trucks. And in a world first — your scooter.

It's called V2V. It could save 90,000 lives a year. And almost nobody knows it's coming.

I'm a college student who fell down this rabbit hole so you don't have to. This is **Kavach** — the whole story, one chapter a week, in plain human language.

## The 30-Second Version

- **1,83,382** people died on Indian roads in 2025 — the highest ever recorded
- **2 out of 3** victims were aged 18–34
- **Every safety feature** in your vehicle today assumes you can see the danger coming
- **V2V removes that assumption** — vehicles warn each other about dangers nobody can see
- **October 2028** it becomes mandatory on every new vehicle in India

Your eyes can't see through buses. Your reflexes can't beat physics. Your car is about to fix both.
